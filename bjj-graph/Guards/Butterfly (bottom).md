## 4 Submission Attacks

1. **Guillotine & Marcelotine:** Snap-down from collar tie, wrap high-elbow grip; finish from mount if sweep fails[91][86].
2. **Armbar / Reverse arm-lock:** Overhook side; extend hip as passer bases to block sweep[23].
3. **Triangle choke & Omoplata:** Overhook pin + cross sleeve; kick leg over shoulder after failed sweep[21].
4. **Leglocks:** Elevation to single-leg-X, outside ashi or 411 saddle via inside-grip entry[94][98].
5. **Calf slicer & Knee-bar:** Inside hook switches to calf crush as opponent back-steps[24].

---
## 5 Transitions & Combinations

* **X-Guard / Single-Leg-X:** When opponent posts a leg to stop sweep, shoot hip under into SLX, then off-balance[54][57].
* **Back Takes:** Arm drag, kick-spin, duck-under or shoulder-crunch roll establish rear control[85][32].
* **Wrestle-Up Series:** Opponent retreats or sprawls; use whizzer to stand into front-headlock or body-lock takedown[97][93].
* **Half-Butterfly to Leg Entries:** Bridge to elevate, shoot bottom leg through for inside-ashi or outside-ashi[87][103].
