This is a position which is great for attacking straight [[Ankle lock]] submission. 



## 1 Position overiew (sitting opponent)
Let's look at more details about this position. Our opponent can sit or stand but for now let's assume he sits. Core of this position is our leg placement. Bottom leg should be inserted as a butterfly hook. It works as both:
* a hook (so it's more difficult for them to back away), 
* a frame (so it's more difficult to come into us).
The second leg goes on top and is placed on their hip. It is a great frame, and when you lift your hips just a bit a lot of weight is transferred through this foot, thus making moving and getting up much more difficult for our opponent. You also should **pinch** your legs together to block opponent's knee in place. 
![[Butterfly ashi (bottom)-1.png]]

Your top arm should operate between opponent's:
* collar,
* sleeve
* knee
all depending on their reactions. 
### 1.1 What are the escaping options?
Generally it's a not good position to be in (for our opponent). They can either remove our top or bottom leg.
#### 1.1.1 Removing top leg
They can remove it with and go to [[Leg drag pass]]. 
![[Butterfly ashi (bottom)-2.png|340]]![[Butterfly ashi (bottom)-3.png|355]]
They can use either left or right arm, but the defense is the same in both cases. Use your top arm to frame on their collar bone (grab their collar). Then try to circle back with your top leg. Your bottom leg should be active the whole time, and should be used to keep the distance between you and your opponent.
![[Butterfly ashi (bottom)-5.png]]
It's very likely that their grip on your leg will be too strong to just circle back with your leg. In such case use your top arm to grab their sleeve and break their grip (by extending trapped leg). Then go back to the hip with your foot.
![[Butterfly ashi (bottom)-4.png]]
They may also decide to push it to the other side
![[Butterfly ashi (bottom)-6.png]]
#### 1.1.2 Removing bottom leg
Another defense is removing your bottom leg
![[Butterfly ashi (bottom)-7.png]]![[Butterfly ashi (bottom)-8.png]]
If you pull your leg towards your butt but they are pulling in opposite direction this allows you to reinsert the hook. If they keep a really strong grip (e.g. on your pants or by pushing your foot on the ground), use your top arm to grab their sleeve and break it.
