## 1 Passing
Usually when passing our first goal is to get rid of the leg on the biceps. After we manage to do so, opponents will usually Lasso with their other leg. If he doesn't do that we can proceed with applying knee cut pass (assuming he has a DLR hook with the other leg). When he does that (which is expected), then successful pass requires dealing with this shallow lasso defense.
