One of the common attacks from bottom is to hold us in a *lockdown lock*. 
![[Half guard (top)-1.png|600]]
You can escape it in many ways, see [instructional video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIkXPK5h-4A&ab_channel=AbsoluteMMAStKilda-Melbourne) for more ideas. Here I will discuss one from Marek coach, it's called "półeczka". The idea is to use your left foot and place it under lockdown hold. Like this:
![[Half guard (top)-2.png|400]]
Remember to be active with it - lift it up to make space under it. Next step is to snap your right leg to the right to free it and immediately do a circular motion and move it under your opponent's legs. Your półeczka should allow you to do that.
